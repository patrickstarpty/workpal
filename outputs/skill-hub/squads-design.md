# Global agent squads

The Agent Team concept presents organization-wide capability through four suggested squads: Discovery & design, Engineering delivery, Verification & review, and Reliability & security. Each has one main-agent lead and named core roles. Researcher, code simplifier, verifier and adversarial reviewing agent form a shared specialist pool, called on demand rather than counted as dedicated members in every squad.

The first view shows all squads, their capabilities, core lineups, shared support and expected deliverables. Opening a squad shows its full composition, triggers, human decisions and artifact handoffs to other squads. Opening a member retains its existing role profile and source-linked suggested skills. All drill-downs move keyboard focus and the viewport to their heading and provide a route back to the originating squad or shared pool.
